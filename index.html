I plan projects with the same instinct I use to art-direct them, and I direct with the same rigor I use to manage them.

In 2025 I co-founded Cape Sante Studio, where I build brand identity and strategic communication for wine, architecture, hospitality, wellness and artisanal food brands.

I got here sideways. A Master’s in International Relations (China Studies, between Turin and Guangzhou) and seven years moving through startups, micromobility, and the tight choreography of executive support in corporate rooms. The creative instinct was always there; it just took a while to stop apologizing for it.

For years I did this work without the title: styling brand moments, art-directing quietly, managing projects that were really about vision as much as logistics.

Cape Sante is where it finally got a name: creative in vision, structured in method.

Photography got there first, actually. I assisted a fashion photographer in Milan, then drifted into documentary work, which I still shoot alongside the studio.

Same question, really, different tools: how identity gets built and shown, in a portrait, a brand, a country’s foreign policy.

I’m always interested in new challenges and projects that bring together creative direction and project management, in Italy, across Europe, or remote.
